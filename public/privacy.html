Privacy Policy

E-Reader Worm · Last updated 4 November 2026

E-Reader Worm is a word puzzle game made by an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: we don't collect your personal information. The phone apps show optional video ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show those ads.

The web version

The web version has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings, saved game and best scores are stored only in your browser on your device. They are never sent to us.

The Android and iPhone apps

Your settings, saved game and best scores are stored only on your device.

The apps can show rewarded video ads, but only when you choose to watch one (for example to get extra shuffles or hints). There are no banner ads and no ads that play without you asking.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your approximate location (based on your IP address),
  • information about how you interact with ads, and app diagnostics.

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can read how Google uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • UK and EU players are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's menu (Privacy settings).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play the game.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play or App Store page.
